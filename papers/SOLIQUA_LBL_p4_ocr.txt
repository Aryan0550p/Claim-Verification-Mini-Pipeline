SOLIQUA® is an fixed-ratio combination of basal insulin glargine (100 units/mL (iGlar)) and the short-acting GLP-1 RA lixisenatide (Lixi).

In the Lixi-LAN study, 1170 patients who had been randomized to SOLIQUA® were matched with 1170 patients who had been randomized to iGlar. In the iGlarLixi group, the mean change in HbA1c was -1.1 (95% CI: -1.2 to -1.0) and in the iGlar group, the mean change was -0.9 (95% CI: -1.0 to -0.9). The LS mean difference was -0.2 (95% CI: -0.3 to -0.2, p<0.001). The mean weight change was -0.4 kg (95% CI: -0.6 to -0.1) in the iGlarLixi group and +1.0 kg (95% CI: 0.8 to 1.3) in the iGlar group. The LS mean difference was -1.4 kg (95% CI: -1.7 to -1.1, p<0.0001). The confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia events were 15.3% in the iGlarLixi group and 15.4% in the iGlar group. The LS mean difference in overall confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia was -0.1 (95% CI: -2.3 to 2.2, p=0.9631). The severe hypoglycemia events were 0.4% in the iGlarLixi group and 0.4% in the iGlar group. The LS mean difference in severe hypoglycemia was 0.0 (95% CI: -0.5 to 0.5, p=0.9696). Overall, the rate ratio for confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia was 1.05 (95% CI: 0.81 to 1.36, p=0.7224). The rate ratio for nocturnal confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia was 1.10 (95% CI: 0.76 to 1.61, p=0.6033). The rate ratio for confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia with a plasma glucose <3.0 mmol/L was 0.94 (95% CI: 0.70 to 1.25, p=0.6728). The rate ratio for nocturnal confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia with a plasma glucose <3.0 mmol/L was 1.04 (95% CI: 0.64 to 1.70, p=0.8653). The rate ratio for confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia with a plasma glucose <2.0 mmol/L was 0.95 (95% CI: 0.61 to 1.48, p=0.8175). The rate ratio for nocturnal confirmed symptomatic hypoglycemia with a plasma glucose <2.0 mmol/L was 0.97 (95% CI: 0.52 to 1.83, p=0.9329).

**Abridged Prescribing Information - Fixed Ratio Combination of Insulin Glargine and Lixisenatide 100U + 50mcg/33mcg SOLIQUA® SoloStar®**
Composition: SOLIQUA® SoloStar® 100U + 50mcg/33mcg contains per mL 100 units insulin glargine and 50/33 mcg of lixisenatide. Indication: SOLIQUA® is indicated for the treatment of adults with diabetes Mellitus with an adjunct to diet and exercise in addition to metformin with or without SGLT2 inhibitors, when this has not been provided by metformin alone or (sulfonylurea, glinide, DPP-4 inhibitors or gliptins, and Sodium-glucose co- transporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors or glifozins) or with basal insulin administration. The starting daily dose of SOLIQUA® is 10 units (10 units of insulin glargine and 5mcg of lixisenatide) and the maximum daily dose of SOLIQUA® is 60 units (60 units of insulin glargine and 20mcg of lixisenatide). The dose should be administered once daily in the hour prior to the first meal. It is preferable that the potential injection of SOLIQUA® is performed before the same meal every day. If the dose of SOLIQUA® is missed, it should be injected within the hour prior to the next meal. The dose of SOLIQUA® should be individualized based on clinical response and patient's need for insulin. SOLIQUA® is titratable and available in two pens (10-40) pen or (30-60) pen, providing different dosing options. SOLIQUA® is to be injected subcutaneously in the abdomen, deltoid, or thigh. The injection sites should be rotated within the same region (abdomen, deltoid, or thigh) on a daily basis. New injection sites should be checked for signs of irritation or inflammation. Patients should be instructed to always use a new needle.
Special Safety Information - Special populations: In pediatric patients below the age of 18 years, safety and effectiveness have not been established. In elderly patients ≥65 years, dose should be adjusted on individual basis. In patients with severe renal impairment or end-stage renal disease, SOLIQUA® is not recommended. In patients with severe gastrointestinal disease, SOLIQUA® is not recommended. In patients receiving oral medicinal products that require rapid gastrointestinal absorption, SOLIQUA® is not recommended. Monitoring of renal function is recommended at regular intervals. Liver impairment: In patients with hepatic impairment, dose adjustment may be necessary. Monitoring of hepatic function is recommended at regular intervals. Pregnancy and Lactation: SOLIQUA® should not be used during pregnancy or breast-feeding. Undesirable effects: The most frequently reported adverse reactions during treatment with SOLIQUA® were hypoglycemia and gastrointestinal disorders. For full prescribing information, please refer to the product leaflet.
**Drug Interactions:**
**Oral Medicinal Products:** Lixisenatide slows gastric emptying, which may reduce the rate of absorption of orally administered drugs. Therefore, drugs that are particularly dependent on gastric emptying for their absorption and drugs that require rapid absorption should be administered at least 1 hour before SOLIQUA® or at least 11 hours after SOLIQUA®.
**Warfarin:** No clinically relevant interaction with warfarin was observed.
**Paracetamol:** The maximum observed paracetamol concentration was reduced by 30% and the overall exposure by 13% with lixisenatide.
**Oral Contraceptives:** Concomitant administration of oral contraceptives with lixisenatide did not affect the pharmacokinetics of ethinyl estradiol and levonorgestrel.
**Insulin:** Insulin-induced hypoglycemia may be increased by lixisenatide.
**Other Antidiabetics:** Concomitant administration of other antidiabetics with lixisenatide did not show any clinically relevant interaction.
**Alcohol:** No interaction with alcohol was observed.
**Hypoglycemia:** Insulin-induced hypoglycemia may be increased by lixisenatide.
**Adverse Reactions:**
The most frequently reported adverse reactions during treatment with SOLIQUA® were hypoglycemia and gastrointestinal disorders.
**Gastrointestinal Disorders:** Vomiting, nausea, diarrhea, and abdominal pain have been reported in clinical trials. These reactions were generally mild to moderate in intensity and transient.
**Hypersensitivity Reactions:** Hypersensitivity reactions (e.g., urticaria, rash, pruritus) have been reported rarely.
**Allergic Reactions:** Allergic reactions (e.g., angioedema, anaphylactic reaction) have been reported rarely.
**Pancreatitis:** Acute pancreatitis has been reported rarely.
**Thyroid Cancer:** Cases of thyroid C-cell hyperplasia and medullary thyroid carcinoma have been reported in animal studies with lixisenatide. The relevance to humans is unknown.
**Injection Site Reactions:** Injection site reactions (e.g., pain, redness, swelling, bruising) have been reported rarely.
**Immunogenicity:** The formation of anti-lixisenatide antibodies may increase the risk of injection site reactions and allergic reactions.
**Cardiac Disorders:** Tachycardia has been reported rarely.
**Renal Impairment:** Renal impairment has been reported rarely.
